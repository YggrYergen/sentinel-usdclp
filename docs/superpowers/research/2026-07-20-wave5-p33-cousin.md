# Wave-5 P33 — V-12 causal cousin (resting pullback-EMA LIMIT, next-bar expiry)

**Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Program:** Honest ("complete the 66"), Wave 5 · **Branch:** `alvaro`

## Question
The DEAD V-12 lever (`entry_timing=1`) filled at `ema_f[i]` tested against bar
`i`'s OWN intrabar low — a level derived from `close[i]`, unknowable until bar i
closes. Its causal-survival excess was **−1.0%..−2.6%** (all negative). The
SIM↔LIVE catalog kept alive ONLY the "P33 pending-order cousin"
(`docs/SIM_VS_LIVE_GAP_ADAPTATION_CATALOG_2026-07-18.md:285,293`). This task
builds the **strictly-causal** cousin and honestly asks: does it recover any edge
over the champion baseline?

## What was built (additive lever `pullback_limit`)
New kwarg `pullback_limit: bool = False` on `simular_variant`
(`sentinel_engine/strategies/emasar_variant.py`). Default `False` = byte-identical
no-op (parity gate sacred; pinned by ≥2-seed + tight-trail edge tests). When
`True` (only from the `entry_timing==0` close-entry signal path):

- A signal at bar `i` does **not** enter at `i`. A resting LIMIT is placed at
  `level = ema_f[i]` (the fast-EMA "pullback" level, legitimately known once bar
  i closes) for bar `i+1` **only**.
- **LONG (BUY LIMIT):** fills iff `bars[i+1].low <= level`, at `price = level`.
- **SHORT (SELL LIMIT):** fills iff `bars[i+1].high >= level`, at `price = level`.
- **Next-bar expiry:** the order lives exactly one bar; unfilled → cancelled (no
  carry past `i+1`). A last-bar signal (no `i+1`) drops unfilled.
- **Strict causality:** the level uses `ema_f[i]` (bar-i close) and the fill uses
  bar `i+1` OHLC **only** — never bar i's own intrabar touch (the V-12 sin).
- Out-of-scope combos (`confirm_bar`, `entry_timing∈{1,2,3}`) raise `ValueError`
  rather than silently mutate those paths. Documented, tested.

Implemented mirroring the sibling deferred-entry lever `confirm_bar` (P54): a
one-bar pending state (`pending_limit_lado`, `pending_limit_level`) captured at
bar i, resolved at bar i+1, never carried further. Initial SL uses the fill
bar's range via the existing `_sl_inicial(lado, i)` call — identical convention
to `confirm_bar` (both fire the entry on the resolution bar).

Offline honest re-score (verbatim `gen_honest_sweep` pricing: `live_fill_mode=True`

- Champion net-series Sharpe (4 windows) = **−0.06**; cousin = **−1.38**.
- Cousin total net is **−9372.60** vs champion **−959.70** — the cousin is
  negative in 3 of 4 windows and **decisively worse overall** (Δ = −8412.90).
- Trade count collapses ~3× (≈1000 → ≈330 per window): most resting LIMITs
  **expire** because next-bar price rarely pulls back through the fast EMA before
  moving on — the fills that do occur are adversely selected.

**Verdict: NO edge recovered.** The strictly-causal cousin does **not** rescue the
DEAD V-12. It is worse than both the champion baseline and the −1%..−2.6% causal
V-12 intrabar figure: the entire apparent V-12 "edge" was the look-ahead of
touching a not-yet-known level on the signal bar itself. Removing the look-ahead
(resting the order into a strictly later bar) removes the edge and then some.

## Statistical honesty
This is a **single config** (one variant, one TF). There is **no trial family**
to deflate against, so any Deflated Sharpe here is **undeflated / meaningless** —
the numbers above are **descriptive only**. No significance is claimed; the
result is negative regardless of deflation, which strengthens (does not weaken)
the "dead lever" conclusion. Read-only against the lake and research.db.
